Calculation assumptions

Review what this estimate includes.

  • Percentage fees and profit margin use post-discount, pre-sales-tax revenue.
  • Marketplace, payment, affiliate and ad rates are combined once; configured fee tax applies to percentage-based fees.
  • Promotional and coupon discounts stack sequentially; order-level fixed fees are treated as one order per unit.
  • Income tax, exemptions and seller-specific shipping rules are not modeled.
